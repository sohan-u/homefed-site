Privacy notice

Dated 9 October 2026

Who we are

Homefed is a proprietorship based in Bengaluru, India. We make the Homefed app. For the personal data the app handles, Homefed is the data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

To reach us about anything in this notice, write to support@homefed.in.

What we collect

  • Your login email.
  • The household you set up: its people's names and profiles, the meal plans, what was cooked and eaten, the pantry and the shopping list.
  • Which phone you use — a device id.
  • Crash records, so we can find and fix what went wrong.

What we never collect

  • Your location.
  • Your contacts.
  • Payment details.
  • Advertising identifiers.

Where it lives

Your household's data is kept on servers in Seoul, South Korea (on Supabase).

Crash records are kept with Sentry in the European Union. Sentry stores no IP addresses for us.

How long we keep it

Your household's data is kept for as long as the household exists. When a household is closed, any adult who was in it can delete it for good. Until the app has its own export, write to support@homefed.in and we'll send you a copy of your household's data.

Crash records are kept for 90 days.

Who sees it

The people in your household. Once the cook's phone exists, the cook will see dishes and the pantry — never a person's data.

Nobody else sees it, and we never sell it.

Your rights

You can ask to see your data, to correct it, to erase it, or to withdraw your consent. Write to support@homefed.in.

If you have a grievance, write to the same address. We will answer it within the time the Act allows.

Children

A child's profile is created and managed by an adult in the household. A child has no login.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date. If a change affects how your data is used, we will tell you before it takes effect.
