Support

Getting help

Write to support@homefed.in. Tell us what you were doing and what you saw, and we'll get back to you.

If your household is in the beta, you can also ask in the WhatsApp group for beta households.

Leaving a household, and taking your data

  1. On the Home page, tap your name at the top right, then Your kitchen.
  2. Until the app has its own export, write to support@homefed.in and we'll send you a copy of your household's data.
  3. Tap Leave this household and confirm.

If you are the last adult in the household, leaving closes it. A closed household can be deleted for good by any adult who was in it.

Deleting your account

During the beta, write to support@homefed.in from the email you sign in with, and ask us to delete your account.

This page is for Homefed 0.1.0, the October 2026 beta.
