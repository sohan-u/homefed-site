Deleting your household

Dated 12 October 2026

What is deleted

Deleting a household deletes its kitchen for good:

  • its people's names and profiles,
  • the meal plans, and what was cooked and eaten,
  • the pantry and the shopping list,
  • the household's invites,
  • and the household's ledger — the record of every change anyone in the household made.

Once it is deleted, we cannot bring it back.

Your login is yours, not the household's, so it stays. If you want your login deleted too, say so when you write to us.

We keep one note that a household was deleted, and when. It has no names in it, and nothing anyone wrote.

Who can delete it

Write to support@homefed.in from the email you sign in with. We send you a copy of the household's data first, and nothing is deleted until you say yes.

How long we keep it

Your household's data is kept for as long as the household exists. When it is deleted, it is gone.

Crash records are kept for 90 days. They are not part of your household.

For everything else about your data, see the privacy notice.
